About the testnet

The eWatts testnet is a live MBPoW (Memory-Bound Proof of Work) network. Blocks are mined every ~600 seconds using DRAM-bound hashing. The protocol emits eWatts proportional to energy consumed by the network, with a bootstrap multiplier that decays as supply approaches 10B eWatts.

Current phase: Testnet — no real value, no exchanges, no wallet downloads. For development and testing only.

What is eWatts?

eWatts is a neutral settlement layer anchored to the physical cost of memory-bound computation. It is the only monetary protocol whose unit (the eWatt) is defined by physics, not by committee. Supply is not capped, but it is also not arbitrary — each eWatt corresponds to real energy spent by the network.

During bootstrap, emission per block is multiplied by up to 100,000x to incentivize early miners. This multiplier decays exponentially toward 1 as cumulative supply approaches the 10B threshold.

At maturity, the network anchors 1 eWatt to approximately $1 USD worth of energy, using the memory-bound nature of MBPoW (GDDR6/DRAM hashing) to keep the production cost floor stable year over year.

Key properties

  • No inflation target — The protocol does not codify an arbitrary inflation rate. Supply growth emerges from network growth.
  • No drift — P_target does not decay over time. The $1/eW anchor is stable.
  • No oracles — No external price feeds, no off-chain dependencies, no governance committees.
  • No founder allocation — No pre-mine, no admin keys, no privileged minting.
  • No supply cap — Hard caps create scarcity pressure that conflicts with settlement function.
  • Memory-bound PoW — DRAM latency dominated. Resistant to ASIC specialization. Stable cost floor.
